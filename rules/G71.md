# G71 — Source units change only through their gated tool

A carve (new or narrowed unit) goes through `tools/carve.py`; a family member's body through `tools/propagate.py --apply` after a green dry run; a standalone draft into its real unit through `tools/reconcile.py --apply`. Each tool builds and byte-gates the alias and restores the tree on failure; hand edits of these units, of `config/c_units.tsv` rows or of generated splat yaml bypass the gate (G3, G61).

Origin: phase 1.6 T3/T4/T5 binding decisions.
