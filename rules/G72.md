# G72 — A task checks its own milestone clause with the verifier's matching rules

Before a task records its milestone clause green, it runs that clause through `PY tools/phaseend_index.py verify`
(on the host), or reproduces exactly what the verifier matches: backticked strings are literal except placeholders,
`a`/`A`/`I` are literal outside a `<X> of <Y>` count slot, and every stated bound (`M ≥ 2`, `B ≥ 7`) is applied.

Why: in phase 1.6, T1, T4, T5 and T6 recorded green against a looser reading of their clause (a detailed control line
instead of the literal `control: ok`, a count of 1 where the bound asked for at least 2, a letter the verifier read
literally). The first phase end was RED 6/10 and needed two extra tasks (T8, T9).

Check: the task summary's `Verified:` line quotes the verifier's GREEN line for its clause.
