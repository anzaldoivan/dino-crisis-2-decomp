# G73 — Describe a residual, never reproduce it: mnemonic names and counts at diff indices only
id: G73 · group: G · status: active · tags: decomp,firewall,plateau · origin: phase 1.7 T7/T9 · added: 2026-10-02

A tool or document may describe a residual against a game function by naming it (`alias:0xADDR`), giving counts, diff
indices and the mnemonic names at those indices: that states the same fact as a lever's `tell` and is within G12. It
may not print or store operands, immediates, encodings, hex words, or a run of consecutive target instructions: those
reproduce the target and cross G12. Applies to stdout that may be pasted into a log or summary as much as to tracked
files. `audit_public.py` is the backstop, not the boundary; the tool's output format is.
provenance: phase 1.7 T7 (plateau.py `evidence:` lines) ruled at T9 and the 1.7 phase end
