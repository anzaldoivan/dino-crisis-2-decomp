## Gotchas for the expert
- workflow: score()'s reconcile probe leaves `.run/reconcile/<alias>_<start>/draft.c` per scored draft; `reconcile.py --check` globs `OUT/*/draft.c`, so failed drafts break the reconcile scanner denominator (`reconcile: 2 of 4 banked without redraft` → scanners 21 of 22). Seen after the scratch gate; fixed by removing the two scratch dirs in the volume. The real M1 gate will hit this too.
- A syntax-error draft at a carve target scores `candidate` (reconcile carve = needs-apply, no compile), bank apply fails → plateau `apply-failed`, plateau.py then labels it UNCOMPILED.
- plateau scratch is `.run/plateau/draft/draft/` for every draft (stem = `draft`); labelling is serial so it is safe.
