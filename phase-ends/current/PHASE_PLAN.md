## Rationale
- Split per the developer's option 1. The milestone keeps the ladder's clauses and scopes the stub and ledger clauses to the exe set, so `0 stubs` is checkable now and the stage and module phases reuse the same `--set` line with their own name.
- New T12 before any campaign wave: the scoped milestone needs a census line that does not yet exist (`--only` drops the fleet and family checks, census.py:886, and does not include overlay copies of exe-headed classes), the developer's usage condition needs a record the wave check can count, and two refusal classes (gte, handasm-marked) keep known walls out of the paid draw. All three are free local work and small; one tool task, one coder. A separate `config/usage.tsv` rather than a new waves.tsv column: no migration of WCOLS (wave.py:450) or of the closed M1 row.
- Usage is read from the harness's usage cache (`~/.claude/usage-ledger/usage_api.json`, `windows.five_hour.pct`, `resets_at`; status.py:243-259 reads the same) on the host; when the cache is stale or absent the expert passes `--pct` from the statusline by hand. A before/after pair whose `resets_at` differ is not a measurement (the window rolled mid-wave) and is recorded as such; the next wave measures again until one pair is valid.
- Order inside the set, leverage-first: T7 exe-headed exact classes first (one bank + propagation banks every overlay copy; ordered by copies × size), then T8 the exe unique pool below the cliff (b1-b5, route `draft`), then T10 the hard lane (b6 residuals, failed drafts, GTE lane, handasm ledger). The first T7 wave is the first campaign wave and carries the usage measurement; after it, T7 re-projects the set's windows from the measured pct and records the arithmetic in its summary (no stop: the developer already chose the split; a projection beyond ~2x the estimate goes to the critic as a `question`).
- GTE lane in T10, not T8: GTE fns need one shared header of cop2 macros (our own text from public mnemonics, no vendor SDK file, GENERATION_PLAN standing constraints) and a whitelist in the verbatim grep (G11); building that once in the hard lane is cheaper than every drafter meeting it.
- T8 stays one task looping waves with handoffs (TASK_PROGRESS.md): the wave ledger, not the task count, is the unit of progress; a split by bucket would add plan churn without a decision point.
- Coder for every task: opus55 (one tier). Drafters: one coder per target in its own work dir (G41). Draft names unique per target (plateau scratch `draft/<stem>` is shared; T5 Next-task-needs).
- All tasks `effort: medium`: preset max5 has no hard rung. `review: yes` on none: every clause is machine-checked.
- No `## Developer decides` items: the split was decided (REPLAN option 1); set definitions, ordering, tooling and wave size follow from T5/T6 and GENERATION_PLAN.md:34.
