## Design notes
- jtbl slice: symbolic eval backwards (≤48 insns, stops at `jr ra`): lui/addiu/ori → const; `sll ,2` → index; addu const+index → table; `lw` off → base. Bound: `sltiu _, src, N` before the sll. Fallback `run`: words pointing into [prev jr ra+8, next jr ra+8), stop at next table start. exe: 58 sltiu, 1 run; overlays 77 sltiu.
- text-end scan = after last `jr ra` + delay slot, ignoring a `jr ra` adjacent to another (exe had a `jr ra; jr ra` pair at 0x800a7338 in data → first naive scan gave 0x800a7344). exe scan = 0x80085f74 = binding; OPTION.BIN scan 0x801c4858 = T5 proven value.
- Ghidra switch extent: first version (consecutive pointer data until next label) merged adjacent unlabelled tables (0x8001830c, 0x8001838c, 0x8001a058) and stopped short at 0x8001ac08 (entry 25 undefined; `sltiu 0x1a` → 26 entries). Fixed: extent = consecutive words whose value ∈ jr's computed targets, stop at next label.
- lib-object: 12 overlapping same-start pairs (identical code in two libs, e.g. LIBCD/LIBDS SYS vs D3_006); both kept. Range 0x8001b3b8..0x80085f74.
