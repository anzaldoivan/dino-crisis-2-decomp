# census.py --check prints fixture totals after the real block
- `census.py --check` prints the real totals first, then the fixture runs' totals (`binaries: 1 of 83`, `phantoms: 1` …).
- A parser of its stdout must take the first block only; a regex over the whole output can bind a fixture value.
