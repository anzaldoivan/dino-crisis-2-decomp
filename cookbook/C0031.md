# Classify uncovered text spans by evidence, not by trimming the range
- A text range taken from the first asm subsegment can still open with data words (a `dlabel` inside `.section .text`).
- Do not trim the range to hide them. Classify each uncovered span: data-in-text when every word is data/invalid per
  spimdisasm, no control-flow target lands in it, and it holds an invalid insn or is referenced as data from outside.
- Data-in-text leaves the denominator and is reported (`data in text: D B (n spans …)`); anything else uncovered is a truncation.
