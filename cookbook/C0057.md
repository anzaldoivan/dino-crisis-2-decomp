# REGALLOC-PERM right after a call: declare the callee non-void

Symptom: v0/v1 (or $2/$3) swapped in the instructions right after a `jal`, the rest of the fn exact.
Cause: with the callee extern `void`, gcc treats $2 as free after the call and allocates the next temp there.
The original callee returns a value, so $2 holds that value and the temp lands elsewhere.

Fix: declare the callee `int` (or the pointer type it returns), even when the caller ignores the value.
When the unit's extern is `void`, align the unit's prototype rather than casting the call.

Proof: lever `nonvoid-callee-return` in config/levers.tsv (slus_012_79:0x8002e54c, before REGALLOC-PERM, after MATCH).
Seen on 5 fns in w41 (T10.c19, c20, c21, c22, c25).
