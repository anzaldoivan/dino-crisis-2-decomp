# C0045 — Byte-field mask/shift order survives combine only when the mask fits andi
tags: codegen-map,combine,mask,shift,andi,extraction,insn-shape · date: 2026-10-01 · phase/task: 1.7/T3 · origin: -

tell: andi-before-srl vs srl-before-andi on a byte field (same two insns, swapped order, different mask)
group: combine

**The residual** — game fn `slus_012_79:0x8001B538` (stores the three bytes of a u32 into two byte tables). Natural
draft `(x >> 8) & 0xff` differs from the game at one insn pair only: the game masks first with `0xff00` and then shifts;
ours shifts then masks with `0xff`. The neighbouring field `(x >> 16) & 0xff` already matches. Reproducer
`tools/probes/levers/repro/combine_mask_shift.c` (our compile, pinned triple):
`a (x & 0xff00) >> 8` → `andi 0xff00; srl 8`; `b (x >> 8) & 0xff` → `srl 8; andi 0xff`;
`c (x & 0xff0000) >> 16` → `srl 16; andi 0xff` (same as b).

**The mechanism** — expand emits the C order verbatim (`and` then `lshiftrt`, or the reverse). combine (try_combine
combine.c:1329) feeds the pair to simplify_shift_const (:8353), which canonicalises `(lshiftrt (and x C) n)` to
`(and (lshiftrt x n) C>>n)`; that is two MIPS insns, so recog fails. combine re-splits a failed result into two insns
only when it combined three (`if (i1 && insn_code_number < 0 ...` combine.c:1878). So:
- `0xff00` fits an `andi` immediate → pair of 2 insns → no split → C order survives. Dump `.combine` of the after draft:
  `(insn 12 10 14 (set (reg/v:SI 82) (lshiftrt:SI (reg:SI 83) (const_int 8 [0x8]))) 316 {lshrsi3_internal1} (insn_list 10 (nil))`
  — LOG_LINK to the `and` insn 10 kept, both insns unchanged from `.cse2`.
- `0xff0000` needs its own constant load → 3 insns (i1 const, i2 and, i3 shift) → find_split_point re-splits the
  canonical form: `.cse2` `(and:SI (reg/v:SI 81) (reg:SI 86))` + `(lshiftrt:SI (reg:SI 85) (const_int 16))` becomes
  `.combine` `(insn 16 … (lshiftrt:SI (reg/v:SI 81) (const_int 16 [0x10])))` + `(insn 18 … (and:SI (reg:SI 85) (const_int 255 [0xff])))`.
  Hence the 16-bit field cannot tell the two spellings apart; the 8-bit field can.
Kit combine levers, translated to 2.95.2 (combine → regmove → sched1 → local_alloc → global):
- u16 type trap: **proven** (reproducer level) as: a u16 temp holding an arithmetic result keeps an `andi 0xffff`
  (combine cannot fold it into a `plus`); an s32 temp does not (`repro/combine_u16_temp.c` c vs d). The lhu+zext fold
  itself is expand, not combine: `(zero_extend:SI (mem/s:HI …))` is already in `.cse2`.
- s16 cast at the load: **proven** (reproducer level): `.combine` merges load + ashift + ashiftrt (3 insns) into
  `(sign_extend:SI (mem/s:HI (plus:SI (reg:SI 4 a0) (const_int 2 [0x2])) 0))) 193 {extendhisi2_internal}` → `lh`;
  a cast after arithmetic keeps `sll 16; sra 16` (`repro/combine_s16_cast.c`).
- RC-12 (`register int zr __asm__("$0"); c = a + zr;`, combine reverses a pinned copy): untested.
- phantom-slot parity (`(use (reg:SI` count in `.combine`): untested.

**The lever** — spell the extraction the way the game orders it: mask-then-shift `(x & 0xff00) >> 8` when the game
shows `andi` before `srl`; shift-then-mask `(x >> 8) & 0xff` when it shows `srl` before `andi`. Only fields whose mask
fits a 16-bit immediate keep the order; a wider mask is canonicalised by combine and either spelling works.

**The byte proof** — `config/levers.tsv` row `mask-then-shift`: `tools/probes/levers/mask-then-shift.before.c`
(`(x >> 8) & 0xff`) differs from the game at word 2; `.after.c` (`(x & 0xff00) >> 8`, the one changed expression) is
byte-identical (relocation-masked) under the pinned triple; `tools/codegen_map.py --check --groups combine` → proven.
