# GTE (cop2) fns: match gcc's register choice with fixed-register and base-register macros

Symptom: a cop2 fn plateaus UNKNOWN/REGALLOC with diffs only around `ctc2`/`swc2`, the rest exact.
Cause: an "r" asm operand lets gcc pick any GPR for the ctc2 source. An "=m" operand lets gcc fold the address.
The original loads three control words into fixed $12-$14 (lw $12..$14, then ctc2 to $0, $2, $4).
It stores with `swc2 $N, off(base)` through a live base register.
Fix (include/gte.h, our text from public mnemonics):
- `GTE_CTC2_024(ptr)`: explicit `lw $12..$14, 0/4/8(%0)` + three ctc2, `$12-$14` in the clobber list.
- `GTE_SWC2_AT(r, off, base)`: `swc2 $r, off(%0)` with `"r"(base)` and a `"memory"` clobber, not `"=m"`.
