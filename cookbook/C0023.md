# splat 0.41: out-of-segment `j` targets missing from undefined_funcs_auto

- Symptom: link of an overlay fails with undefined symbols for `j` targets outside the overlay's own address range (e.g. into the exe or another overlay); splat 0.41.0 lists `jal` targets in `undefined_funcs_auto.txt` but omits these.
- Fix: generate the missing symbols (`<name> = 0x<addr>;`) into an extra linker-script include (here make-generated `undefined_jumps_auto.txt`) from the split `.s`.
- Source: phase 1.3 T4 (bin_wep_s07); Makefile rule.
