# C0048 — Param def sinks to its use in sched1 (birthing boost): give the pseudo a 2nd SET (++n) to keep it on top
tags: codegen-map,sched,sched1,sched2,birthing,insn-order,param,sched-reorg · date: 2026-10-02 · phase/task: 1.7/T4 · origin: -

tell: narrowed-param andi+addiu sunk below the constant loads vs leading the fn (same insns, reordered)
group: sched-reorg

**The residual** — game fn `slus_012_79:0x8003E164` (struct init: 13 constant stores and one `s->f158 = n + 1` of a
byte param). Natural draft (`int n; n &= 0xff; ... s->f158 = n + 1;`, or a `u8 n` param) has the game's 20 insns, same
registers, but the param's `andi`+`addu` sit after the first two `li`s instead of leading the fn: a pure reorder, words
0-3. Reproducer `tools/probes/levers/repro/sched_birth_jal.c` (cc1 `.s`, before maspsx):
`birth_c (n + 1)` → `li $2,7 | li $3,9 | andi $5,$5,0x00ff | addu $5,$5,1 | sw … | sw … | j $31 | sw $5,12($4)`;
`birth_d (++n)` → `andi $5,$5,0x00ff | addu $5,$5,1 | li $2,7 | li $3,9 | sw … | sw … | j $31 | sw $5,12($4)`.
Same with a call: `birth_a g(n + 1)` puts the `andi` right above the `jal`; `birth_b g(++n)` leads the fn.

**The mechanism** — the pinned cc1 runs the old list scheduler `gcc/sched.c`, not haifa: 2.95.2 configure.in:3552-3557
enables haifa only for alpha/hppa/powerpc/rs6000/sparc/m32r, and the `.sched` dump prints sched.c's own strings
(`;; ready list at T-%d:` sched.c:3198, `insn %d has a greater potential hazard` :2140). It schedules bottom-up from the
block end. When a consumer is scheduled, adjust_priority (sched.c:1958) raises a ready producer to `max_priority` if
birthing_insn_p (:1922) holds: a SET of a REG that is live and has `REG_N_SETS == 1` (sched1 only; returns 0 once
reload_completed). The raised insn is taken next, i.e. placed directly above its consumer: the def sinks to its use.
- draft (`n + 1`): after combine, pseudo 82 = `zero_extend (subreg:QI a1)` is set once (the entry copy folded in), the
  `plus` writes a fresh pseudo 86. `.sched`: `;; ready list at T-7: … 48 (7f000001), now 48 45 …` and
  `;; ready list at T-8: … 12 (7f000001), now 12 45 …` → `(insn 12 45 48 …)`: the andi (12) and addu (48) sink to the
  store (50). sched2 then relays that order (LUID = post-greg order) and puts the two `li`s (15, 20) first:
  `.sched2` `(insn 12 20 48 …)`.
- lever (`++n`): the `plus` writes pseudo 82 itself → `REG_N_SETS(82) == 2` → no boost: `.sched` T-7 shows `48 (1)`,
  the potential-hazard rule (schedule_select :2070) keeps taking stores, and 12, 48 float to the block top:
  `.sched` order `12 48 4 15 17 …`; `.greg` keeps it; `.sched2` `(insn 12 4 48 …)` → andi, addu, li, li (game order).
rank_for_schedule (sched.c:1837): priority (:1848) > class vs last scheduled insn, 3 classes (:1851-1876) > lower LUID
(:1881). No REG_WEIGHT, interblock or depend_count rule (those are haifa-sched.c:4137-4224, inactive here).

**The lever** — when a value derived from a param (or any single-use temp) sits right above its use in our build but
leads the block in the game, give its pseudo a 2nd SET: update the variable in place (`++n`, `n += k`, `n &= 0xff` then
`++n`) instead of a fresh expression (`n + 1`). A fresh local (`int m = n; ... ++m` on a `u8 n`) did not match (diff at word
0; not dumped). Inverse: a def that leads in ours but sits at its use in the
game wants a single-set expression.

**The byte proof** — `config/levers.tsv` row `multi-set-param`: `tools/probes/levers/multi-set-param.before.c`
(`s->f158 = n + 1;`) differs from the game at word 0 (reorder of words 0-3); `.after.c` (`s->f158 = ++n;`, the one
changed expression) is byte-identical (relocation-masked) under the pinned triple;
`tools/codegen_map.py --check --groups regalloc,combine,sched-reorg` → proven.
