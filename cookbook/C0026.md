# Pin a toolchain by output equivalence classes, not by triples

When several toolchain triples match every probe, assembler-version tells that the probes never exercise make
triples indistinguishable (e.g. aspsx 2.56-2.86 on gp-free, `-G0` code; gcc-2.95.2-psx vs psyq4.6 differing only in
CRLF of the .s). Group the matching triples by their output (raw function words + reloc list per probe) and count
classes: "exactly one triple" means one class. Pick the class member by a stated tie rule (era-native build) and
record the rule; otherwise the ladder never reaches 1. tools/probe.py prints the `class` lines.
