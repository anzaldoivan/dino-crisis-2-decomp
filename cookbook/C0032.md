# Ghidra BinaryLoader on PSX overlays: seed functions in a rolled-back txn
- The raw BinaryLoader finds almost no functions in a PSX overlay.
- Seed jal targets, Ghidra refs and pointer words inside a transaction that is rolled back after the dump.
- Decompile `jr $reg` functions (switch recovery) before pointer-word seeding, otherwise jump-table case labels become functions.
- Source: Phase 1.5 T2 (tools/ghidra/scripts/DumpFunctions.java, tools/ghidra/dump_functions.sh).
