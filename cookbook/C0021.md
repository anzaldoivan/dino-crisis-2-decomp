# Keep non-generated artifacts out of build/

- `make clean` that does `rm -rf build` also deletes ignored, hand-made or long-running outputs parked under `build/` (here `build/ghidra_rebuild/`).
- Fix: `clean` removes only the per-unit generated outputs it knows (asm/, build/overlays.mk, build/<alias>/, build/<alias>.{bin,elf,map,ld,override.yaml,...}); anything else under build/ survives.
- Better still: park non-generated artifacts outside build/ (e.g. `.run/`).
- Source: phase 1.3 T3 gotcha, T4 Makefile clean rule; docs/ops/docker-host.md `clean`.
