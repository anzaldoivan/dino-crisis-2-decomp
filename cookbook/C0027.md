# `make -n` checks also see recipe comments and case branches

`make -n` echoes the whole recipe text, including shell comments and unexecuted `case` branches. A check like
"no `c.o` in `make -n build`" therefore fails on a comment or a dormant branch that mentions the literal. Keep the
literal out of the recipe text (build it from variables), or check the produced files instead of the dry run.
