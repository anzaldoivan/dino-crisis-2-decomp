# dumpsxiso -x puts non-ISO files in the output dir

dumpsxiso writes `license_data.dat` (the system area) and Track 2 audio (`*.WAV` for `da` entries) inside its `-x` directory.
Move them out before diffing against an ISO-tree extractor, or the diff reports them as Only-in.
