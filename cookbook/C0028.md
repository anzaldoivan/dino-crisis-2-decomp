# splat 0.41 `c` subsegments can emit stub C bodies

When a segment gets a `c` subsegment, splat64 0.41 writes `INCLUDE_ASM` lines for most functions but stub C bodies
for some (small or odd ones). A stub compiles and silently breaks the match. After generating, check every new .c
for lines other than `#include` and `INCLUDE_ASM(...)` and replace stubs with `INCLUDE_ASM` before the first build.
